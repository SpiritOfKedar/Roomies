How a turn works, in 3 steps

  1. Roomies picks the next roommate

    It skips whoever did the chore last and anyone holding two open chores, then picks who has done it least lately.

  2. You hand in a photo

    Tick the chore, attach a photo and add a line if the job needs one. The turn moves to In review.

  3. The house votes on it

    Most of the other roommates have to approve. Rejected work comes back to you, and you can hand it in 3 more times.

Questions

What roommates ask before they move their chores over.

Who decides whose turn it is?

Roomies does. It skips the last person to do the chore and anyone with two open chores, then picks who has done it least and longest ago.

Can I vote on my own chore?

No. The other active roommates vote and a majority decides. In a house of four, that is two approvals.

What if my photo is rejected?

The turn comes back as Changes requested. You can hand it in 3 more times before it fails and costs points.

What happens when a deadline passes?

The turn is marked Missed and the chore's penalty is added. A repeating chore then moves to the next roommate.

Can a bill be split unevenly?

Not today. Every bill is split equally between the roommates who are active in the house.

How do my roommates join?

Start the house, then send them its invite code. They sign up, enter the code and join the rotation.
